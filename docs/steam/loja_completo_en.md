## Short description

Turn Dona Dilce's kiosk into the Maré Alta resort. Serve beachgoers, manage on your phone, expand to the Grand Hotel and care for the village as you work towards repaying a R$ 1.000.000 debt.

## About the game

### Your inheritance came with a sea view. And a bill.

Dona Dilce has moved in with her bingo friends and handed you the family kiosk. Along with it came a letter,
childhood memories and a **R$ 1.000.000** debt. Start with your first chilled coconuts and turn **Maré Alta** into
a lively resort in **Coconut Coast: Resort Simulator**.

You are here to work, meet people and build a business of your own. Along the way are guests asking for another
night, neighbours with plans for the village and that customer who is certain the wind should be kinder to their hair.

### Serve up close. Manage wherever you are.

At the kiosk, take orders, collect ready-made products and deliver them at the counter. Payment arrives on delivery.
Restock, improve recipes and decoration, and hire an attendant to help with the routine.

Use your phone to track cash, staff, improvements and objectives. Choose between investing in the next business,
refining what you have and progressing with debt repayments. The million is a family commitment, with no interest
or compulsory minimum instalment. A village celebration does not mean the bill has been paid.

### From your first inn to the Grand Hotel

Build a routine around the inn, pool, **Bar do Coqueiro** and coastal tours. Mix drinks, negotiate room rates,
care for rooms and guide visitors at sea or along the lighthouse trail.

The **Grand Hotel** expands your resort with **32 rooms across three categories**. Three visitable interiors bring
you closer to housekeeping, minibars, complaints and VIP guest requests. The wider team helps run the hotel.

### The village is part of the business

Follow the chapters of Seu Severino, Padre Robson, Dona Cecília and Mayor Tuanny. Fish, photograph the scenery,
help your neighbours and develop five businesses: an ice cream shop, pastel food truck, board and kayak rentals,
souvenir shop and surf school.

In the hotel restaurant, serve tables and room orders, use fish from the pier and try to impress the food critic.
Set up pieces for beach weddings, birthdays and conferences before the guests arrive.

After work, the game room offers pool, foosball, table tennis, **Corrida de Jangada**, **Pinball do Farol** and
**Campeonato da Maré**. A good excuse for just one more game.

### What changes from the demo

The main game's Early Access build lets you progress beyond **“A Maré Alta da vila”**, open the Grand Hotel and
game room, explore hotel activities and work towards becoming debt-free. The demo is a free introduction;
the main game remains in development, with its available features described on this page.

During Early Access, our priority is to refine progression, clarity, presentation, stability and performance
with community reports. Read the Early Access answers for the current state and priorities.
