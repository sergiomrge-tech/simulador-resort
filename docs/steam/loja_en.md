## Short description

Inherit a beach kiosk and start turning Maré Alta into a resort. Serve customers, manage your business on your phone and meet a village full of stories in this free demo of Coconut Coast: Resort Simulator.

## About this demo

### A kiosk, a family and your first tide

Dona Dilce has moved in with her bingo friends. She left you the kiosk, plenty of love and a debt of **R$ 1.000.000**.
Now it is your turn to name the business, flip the counter sign and welcome your first customers.

The free demo of **Coconut Coast: Resort Simulator** introduces the daily life of Maré Alta and its neighbours.
Work behind the counter, discover activities along the coast and help a Brazilian village where a chat may turn into
a family recipe, a beach celebration or a fishing story that has grown since yesterday.

### Hand over a product, receive the payment

Take the order, collect a ready-made product and deliver it. Payment happens on delivery. At the kiosk, products
already come prepared, leaving you to focus on service, supplies and keeping the business running. Hire an attendant
to help when you want to explore the village.

Your phone brings together stock, staff, finances, recipes and improvements. Follow missions and decide where to
invest the next money in your till.

### Life beyond the counter

- Open **Bar do Coqueiro**, mix ingredients and discover drinks.
- Welcome guests at the inn, negotiate nightly rates and tend to the bungalows between stays.
- Keep the pool clean and sell the popsicles requested by bathers.
- Sell tickets and lead boat, snorkelling and lighthouse-trail tours. Weather and progression affect departures.
- Get to know the ice cream shop, pastel food truck, board and kayak rentals, souvenir shop and surf school.
- Fish from the pier, photograph the coast and help local residents through the village chapters.

Mixing drinks and running village businesses involve their own tasks. Ready-made products describe the kiosk;
each activity has its own way to serve customers.

### About two hours to get started — and freedom to stay

The opening progression is designed for about **2 hours**, depending on your pace. The campaign reaches its next
step, **“A Maré Alta da vila”**, which requires the Grand Hotel and belongs to the main game.

The demo's thank-you screen appears after its available expansions and village chapters. You may keep playing and
look after your resort: reaching two hours does not force you to stop.

### The next tide is in the main game

The **Grand Hotel**, rooms with special requests, restaurant, buildable beach events and game room belong to the
main game. Paying off Dona Dilce's debt is also part of that continuation. Completing the demo does not require
repaying the million.
