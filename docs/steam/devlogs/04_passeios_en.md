# Devlog 4 — Beyond the counter: tours and the coast

> Editorial development log, without a promised release date. Use only approved, real game captures; never present placeholders as gameplay.

Running Maré Alta means more than standing behind a till. Boat trips, guided snorkelling and walks to the lighthouse connect the resort economy to the landscape and the village.

Passengers need to board, collect life vests and follow the route. At the reef, fish and corals should be part of real gameplay, not a marketing image the game cannot reproduce.

We're checking weather, schedules, NPC movement and tour rewards. Exploring the coast is part of the job and the story.

**Follow the demo and wishlist Coconut Coast: Resort Simulator on Steam.**
