# Devlog 6 — Life at the pool — and a waterslide down the road

> Editorial development log, without a promised release date. Use only approved, real game captures; never present placeholders as gameplay.

Building a pool should make the resort feel more alive. Guests need to arrive at the right time, get in the water, swim, chat or float. Players also handle cleaning, towels and ice pops.

For the full game's Grand Hotel, we're working on guests climbing and riding the waterslide. Bodies, hair, splashdown and pool boundaries all need real testing before this becomes a marketing promise.

The demo includes the guesthouse pool, **not the Grand Hotel waterslide**. We will use actual game captures with working swimmers.

**Follow the demo and wishlist Coconut Coast: Resort Simulator on Steam.**
