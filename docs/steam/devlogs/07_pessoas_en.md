# Devlog 7 — Making the village feel inhabited

> Editorial development log, without a promised release date. Use only approved, real game captures; never present placeholders as gameplay.

A resort needs people who feel present. MetaHuman helps with expressive faces, but visuals alone are not enough: villagers must walk, arrive at the right places, talk and react to the player.

We're reviewing outfits for each role, movement, collisions, and how hair and hands behave. The village also has smaller dramas and comic moments, including Seu Tibúrcio's stories.

Promotional images will only be approved when they show **real characters in the build**, not posed stand-ins hiding problems.

**Follow the demo and wishlist Coconut Coast: Resort Simulator on Steam.**
