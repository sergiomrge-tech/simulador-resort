# Devlog 5 — From bungalow to Grand Hotel

> Editorial development log, without a promised release date. Use only approved, real game captures; never present placeholders as gameplay.

The guesthouse changes how you work with visitors: negotiate nightly rates, keep rooms clean and look after guests. It is more than a passive income source.

The **full game** continues toward the Grand Hotel: 32 rooms across three categories, with three visitable interiors, VIP requests, a restaurant and room service. This **does not mean 32 unique room layouts**.

The free demo ends before this phase. We'll only market the Grand Hotel as a release feature after it is integrated and tested in the full build.

**Follow the demo and wishlist Coconut Coast: Resort Simulator on Steam.**
