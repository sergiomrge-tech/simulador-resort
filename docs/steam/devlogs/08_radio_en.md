# Devlog 8 — The radio plays; the ocean stays

> Editorial development log, without a promised release date. Use only approved, real game captures; never present placeholders as gameplay.

We want the kiosk to have its own sound. The house, kiosk and hotel have radio sets; the management phone is intended to provide previous, next and off controls. We're also planning a **Disable music** option that keeps ocean, rain and sound effects audible.

Some tracks are original compositions synthesised for the game. We're reviewing 12 more from a CC0 library: **commercial permission does not guarantee freedom from Content ID claims on streams**. These tracks are not cleared to advertise as stream-safe.

Before release we need to check station navigation, persistent audio settings and licensing. The beach should still sound like a beach when music is off.

**Follow the demo and wishlist Coconut Coast: Resort Simulator on Steam.**
