# Devlog 3 — Mixing things up at Bar do Coqueiro

> Editorial development log, without a promised release date. Use only approved, real game captures; never present placeholders as gameplay.

The kiosk serves ready-made products, but Bar do Coqueiro works differently: choose ingredients, put the drink together and serve the finished order. Two distinct activities keep resort management varied.

We're refining hand animations, counter placement and order readability. Mixing a drink should feel like an activity, not a meaningless series of clicks.

The demo introduces this expansion without forcing you to forget the kiosk. More content will come as the full game develops.
