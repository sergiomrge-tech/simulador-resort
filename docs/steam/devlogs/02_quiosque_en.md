# Devlog 2 — Starting small: a kiosk you actually run

> Editorial development log, without a promised release date. Use only approved, real game captures; never present placeholders as gameplay.

Before hotels and major expansions, there is a counter, an OPEN sign, and your first customers. Take an order, pick up the ready-to-serve product, and hand it over. **The customer pays on delivery**, not when ordering and not twice.

Your phone handles stock, staff, recipes, cash and expansions. An attendant can help while you focus elsewhere, but hiring costs and wages still need to balance.

The demo is about starting with little and making the place work. We're checking initial progression, saving and whether the routine makes sense without an endless tutorial.

**Follow the demo and wishlist Coconut Coast: Resort Simulator on Steam.**
