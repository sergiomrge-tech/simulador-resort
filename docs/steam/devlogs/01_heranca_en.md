# Devlog 1 — A kiosk, a grandma, and a million in debt

> Editorial development log, without a promised release date. Use only approved, real game captures; never present placeholders as gameplay.

Maré Alta begins with an unusual inheritance: Dona Dilce has moved in with her bingo friends, leaving behind a seaside kiosk, an affectionate letter, and **R$ 1,000,000 in debt**. She's alive and has a story of her own. Your long-term goal is to rebuild the family business.

The free demo introduces the letter, kiosk management and the village. Paying off the entire debt and finishing that storyline belong to the full game, subject to integration and testing. No interest or mandatory instalments are planned: how you invest or save is your decision.

We want a distinctly Brazilian story with warmth and humour, backed by management choices that matter.

**Follow the demo and wishlist Coconut Coast: Resort Simulator on Steam.**
