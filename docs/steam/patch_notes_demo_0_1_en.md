## Welcome to Maré Alta

Your first tide starts at Dona Dilce's kiosk. Serve visitors, manage your business on your phone and discover
the stories of a Brazilian beach village.

### In this demo

- Kiosk service with ready-made products and payment on delivery.
- An inn, Bar do Coqueiro, pool and coastal tours.
- Village chapters, fishing, photography and businesses with their own tasks.
- About two hours of opening progression, depending on your pace. You can continue after the thank-you screen.

### Text and guidance

- Shorter first-time tips with updated instructions for service, the bar, tours and housekeeping.
- Guidance for doors and rereading Dona Dilce's letter.
- Clearer mission text about delivered products and sales during each step.
- Consistent names and terminology, with revised translations across the 12 available languages.
- More dialogue variations during beach incidents.
- An ending message explaining the story's continuation and the family debt.

### Where the story continues

The Grand Hotel, hotel activities, game room and paying off the debt belong to the main game. The demo introduces
that journey and can stay open after its thank-you screen. Repaying the debt is not required to complete the demo.
