## Long description

Grandma Dilce has moved in with her bingo friends. She left you the family kiosk, a letter full of love, and one detail she never quite got around to mentioning over lunch: **R$ 1,000,000 in debt**.

In **Coconut Coast: Resort Simulator**, every chilled coconut helps build the next chapter of **Maré Alta**. Take orders, pick up ready-to-serve products and deliver them at the counter. Restock, manage finances from your phone, hire help and choose your next investment. Between shifts, get to know a Brazilian seaside village whose residents always have another story — or favour — to share.

The free demo introduces the first stage: your kiosk, a guesthouse, the Bar do Coqueiro, a pool, tours and village businesses, unlocked as you progress. Learn about your promise to Grandma and start balancing business investment against voluntary debt payments. **Paying off the debt is the full game's story goal**, with no interest, mandatory instalments or penalty for putting a payment off. You do not need to repay the million to finish the demo.

The planned Early Access continuation leads to the Grand Hotel, its restaurant and more resort activities. Your family handed you a debt. What will you leave to the village?

**Wishlist Coconut Coast: Resort Simulator on Steam.**

## Key features

- Hands-on kiosk service: take orders, collect ready products, restock and get paid on delivery.
- Portable management of supplies, staff, finances, upgrades and expansions.
- Grow a seaside counter into a resort while staying close to your customers.
- Residents, family stories, fishing, photography and Brazilian seaside humour.
- Tours and village businesses with their own activities as you unlock them.
- A campaign-wide goal: repay **R$ 1,000,000** at your own pace.

## Story and tone

The letter brings the love before it brings the bill. Grandma Dilce is alive: this is not a story about her death. It is about carrying a family dream forward while Grandma still asks for an ocean-view room and a family discount. Repayment milestones offer narrative recognition, not free money to pay off the same debt. The village celebration and the final repayment are separate milestones.

## FAQ

**Is it free?** The demo will be free. The full-game price has not been announced.

**Does the demo stop after two hours?** Two hours is the progression target, not a shutdown timer. You can keep running the unlocked businesses after the summary.

**Do I need to repay a million in the demo?** No. The demo introduces the goal. Full repayment and its ending belong to the full game. The planned demo payment cap is R$ 250,000 in total.

**Is there interest or a debt-related game over?** The approved design has voluntary payments, no interest, minimum instalments or late fees. It must be implemented and tested before this material is published.

**Is it multiplayer?** The announced scope is single-player.

**Will demo saves carry over?** Save transfer between the demo and full game has not been confirmed; it needs testing across App IDs.

**What about achievements and Steam Cloud?** Achievements are specified. Steam publication, offline handling and service configuration still require integration and testing; they are not certified features here.

**When is it coming out?** No release date is announced in this kit.
